**Open design question raised by this card, for the user, not for a worker:** `api/directory.rs` now gates directory registration on `config.registration.mode`, as instructed, but the worker flags that a discovery document's `registration_mode` conflates two different things — a server's own registration policy (open/invite/closed) and whether it advertises itself as openly joinable. On the current shape a factory-invite-only server silently stops advertising open directory registration. Its proposed shape is a distinct `discovery.open_registration: bool`. Not decided here.
